15 g / 250 g · Foundation

Two pours. One consistent routine.

01 / Bloom · 100 g

Pour at 94°C and 10 g/s. Use 50 mm circles. For circles, aim for 1.5–2 per second. Keep the spout parallel and 8–10 cm from the vessel.

02 / First drawdown · 35–60 seconds

After the bloom pour, allow 35–60 seconds for the slurry front to descend. The wall layer grows behind it and the bottom completes as the front reaches the base, leaving only a small slurry cap.

03 / Finish · another 150 g

Repeat the same pouring action at 94°C and 10 g/s. Add the remaining 150 g in 15 seconds, stopping at 250 g total. Fresh water rotates into the bottom bed and up the sides.

Bold extraction · 21.5–22.5% EYTDS · 1.40–1.50% · aim 1.45%Last drips · 2:40–2:55

Dial in your grind

Start a little finer than your normal pour-over grind. If the brew finishes before 2:40, grind finer. If it finishes after 2:55, grind coarser. Check the bloom drawdown as well as the final drip time.
